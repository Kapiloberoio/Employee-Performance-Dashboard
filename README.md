Employee Performance Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

1. Project Overview

The **Employee Performance Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze employee performance across key metrics, including productivity, efficiency, and attendance.

The dashboard helps HR professionals and management monitor workforce performance, identify improvement opportunities, and make data-driven decisions related to employee development and workforce optimization.

2. Project Objectives

- Analyze employee productivity, efficiency, and attendance.
- Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Compare performance across departments and job roles.
- Identify performance gaps and potential areas for improvement.
- Support data-driven HR and workforce management decisions.

3. Key Features

- Interactive Visualizations: Charts and graphs for exploring employee performance trends.
- KPI Tracking: Monitor productivity, efficiency, and attendance metrics.
- Department-Wise Analysis: Compare performance across departments and job roles.
- Filters and Drilldowns: Analyze data by department, role, and time period.
- DAX Measures: Create custom calculations and performance indicators.
- Data Transformation: Clean, transform, and prepare data using Power Query.

4. Key Insights & Analysis

The dashboard is designed to provide insights into different aspects of employee performance.

- Productivity Analysis: Evaluates employee productivity and helps identify high-performing employees and areas that may require improvement.
- Efficiency Analysis: Compares employee efficiency across departments and job roles to understand differences in workforce performance.
- Attendance Analysis: Examines employee attendance patterns and helps investigate their potential relationship with productivity.
- Department-Wise Performance: Enables management to compare departments and identify areas performing well or requiring additional support.
- Performance Monitoring: Tracks key performance indicators to help HR teams monitor workforce effectiveness over time.

*Note: These describe the analyses supported by the dashboard. Specific findings should be added after validating the actual dashboard data.*

5. Business Impact

The dashboard supports HR and management teams in the following ways:

- Performance Improvement: Identifies performance gaps and opportunities for employee development.
- Workforce Optimization: Supports better workforce planning and resource allocation.
- Data-Driven Decision-Making: Provides a consolidated view of employee performance metrics.
- Attendance Monitoring: Helps identify attendance patterns that may require further investigation.
- Strategic HR Planning: Supports performance reviews and employee development initiatives.

9. How to Use

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the `.pbix` file using Power BI Desktop.
3. Refresh the dataset and verify the data source connections, if required.
4. Use filters, slicers, and drilldowns to explore the dashboard.
5. Analyze performance indicators to understand employee and department-level trends.
